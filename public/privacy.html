PLAYER INFORMATION / PLAYTEST

Privacy notice

Last updated October 4, 2026. This notice describes what the current BLACKGRID browser playtest handles. It has guest names, room chat, and optional voice. It does not currently have player accounts or a friends list.

Information used during play

Guest name and game state. The name you enter, your room, and the live match state are held in the game server while you are connected. Your room shares your displayed name and in-game state with its participants.

Room messages. Text you send is relayed to people in the same room. The game server does not save room messages as a chat history. Other players may still copy, screenshot, or record what they see.

Voice. Voice is off until you press the microphone button and grant browser permission. Voice audio is sent between players using WebRTC. BLACKGRID's server coordinates the connection but does not receive or record the voice stream. Your browser can send network connection details to other voice participants as part of WebRTC connection setup. The game uses Google's public STUN service to help establish direct connections.

Connection data. The game server reads your network address to enforce connection limits and protect the service. The hosting provider may process request and security logs under its own policies.

Information stored in your browser

BLACKGRID saves your callsign and gameplay settings, such as graphics, volume, and motion preference, in local browser storage on your device. This playtest does not use that storage to create an online account. You can remove it by clearing this site's browser data.

How long information remains

Live player and room data is held in server memory while an operation is active. Empty rooms are removed after the last player leaves. Chat messages and voice audio are not stored by the game application. Browser settings remain on your device until you clear them. Hosting-provider logs follow the provider's retention rules.

Who receives information

People in your operation receive your callsign, game state, and messages. Voice is shared with human players in your room when your microphone is live; AI characters do not receive it. Render hosts the current public playtest. WebRTC connection setup uses a public STUN service. Do not enter private or sensitive information in a callsign or room chat.

Your choices

  • Choose a callsign that does not identify you personally.
  • Leave an operation to stop sharing live game state with that room.
  • Press the microphone button again to mute. You can also revoke microphone permission in your browser's site settings.
  • Clear this site's local browser data to remove saved callsign and settings.

Accounts and children

This playtest has no sign-in, saved online account, friend list, or account deletion workflow. Do not use a callsign or message to share personal information. If you are a child, ask a parent or guardian before using online chat or voice.

Changes and contact

This notice will change if the game adds accounts, durable profiles, analytics, or new data handling. This playtest has no dedicated privacy email configured. For a privacy question, contact the person who shared the playtest link with you. A public release should publish a direct operator contact before it collects account information.
